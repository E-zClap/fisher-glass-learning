\textbf{Regularization changes both information and accuracy.}
End-to-end Waterbirds training with MobileNetV3-small and Group DRO plus Fisher-Glass. Points and bars show means and standard deviations across five seeds at six evaluated regularization coefficients. Dashed lines show the historical Group DRO reference from the $\lambda_{\FG}=10^{-4}$ sweep cohort; it is not a common paired baseline across coefficients. Increasing the penalty raises the Fisher-Glass certificate over much of the range, while strong penalties reduce predictive accuracy. Connecting segments guide the eye.
