(A) Uncentered feature--Schur fractions for the two eligible slices, with selected waterbird/water in blue. Eligibility requires accuracy at least the overall validation accuracy and NLL at most the median across slices; eligibility and selection are evaluated separately within each seed. (B) Held-out accuracy on the frozen selected slice: original images, same-type background control, and opposite-type replacement. (C) Controlled accuracy drops (same-type minus opposite-type) across all four groups; negative values indicate improvement under the opposite-type replacement. (D) The selected slice's controlled drop for ERM and validation-selected ERM+FG. Each model and slice is frozen before test interventions; the extracted bird foreground and labels are preserved. Points and bars show means and reported nominal 95\% normal-approximation intervals ($1.96$ standard errors) across five seeds, 22--26. The selected-slice ERM drop is $52.6\pm1.4$ percentage points. The ERM+FG comparison is not accuracy-matched in two of five seeds.
