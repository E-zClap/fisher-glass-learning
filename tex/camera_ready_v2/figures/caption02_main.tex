\textbf{Synthetic illustration of the geometric tail law in Theorem~\ref{thm:inverse-information-geometry}}.
(A) The surviving score has norm $\delta$ and information $\lambda=\delta^2$. (B) Raw information stays at least one while projected information approaches zero. (C) Empirical inverse-information survival (blue) follows the theoretical $x^{-1/2}$ tail (dashed). B--C share one regenerated seed-11 draw of 300,000 environments, $\delta\sim\mathrm{Uniform}(0,0.35)$ and $L=(\delta^2+10^{-12})^{-1}$. The separately reported five-seed exponent is $0.498\pm0.003$ (nominal 95\% normal-approximation interval, $\pm1.96$ standard errors).
