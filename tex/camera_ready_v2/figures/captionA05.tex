\textbf{Acquisition combines a geometric score with current task loss.}
Changes in held-out worst-group accuracy and Fisher-Glass certificate after feature-space counterfactual acquisition on Waterbirds, at $\gamma_{\mathrm{cf}}=1$. The empirical $\widetilde{\TTI}_\alpha$ multiplies the implemented normalized residual score $\widetilde G_\alpha$ by current loss; the controls use high loss or the residual score alone (Appendix~\ref{app:feature-acquisition}). Points are reported means across five seeds, 17--21. Budgets are percentages of training-set size, with candidates selected from validation features. The source summary supplies no uncertainty intervals. Connecting segments guide the eye between the three evaluated budgets.
