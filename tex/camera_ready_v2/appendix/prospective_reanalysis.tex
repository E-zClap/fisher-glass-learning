\paragraph{Centering and predictive-Fisher checks.}
This post hoc reanalysis retains the five frozen checkpoints, validation coordinates $v_i=(z_i,b_i)^\top$, global scales, temperatures, original eligible sets, and existing intervention outcomes. No model or probe is refitted. Task coordinate $z$ retains training-logit normalization; nuisance coordinate $b$ retains the global validation normalization of the out-of-fold probe. Write $\langle\cdot\rangle_g=n_g^{-1}\sum_{i\in g}(\cdot)$ and compare
\[
J_g=\langle vv^\top\rangle_g,\qquad
\Sigma_g=\langle(v-\mu_g)(v-\mu_g)^\top\rangle_g,\qquad
\mu_g=\langle v\rangle_g.
\]
Centering uses divisor $n_g$ and does not rescale the coordinates. For the predictive check, specify the local Bernoulli model
\[
\operatorname{logit}p_{\theta,\xi}(x)=f_w(x)/\widehat T+\theta z(x)+\xi b(x),
\qquad (\theta,\xi)=(0,0).
\]
Its likelihood score is $(Y-p(x))v(x)$, with conditional predictive mean zero even when $v$ has nonzero slice mean. The resulting predictive Fisher and common surviving fraction are
\[
I_g=\langle qvv^\top\rangle_g,\quad q=p(1-p),\qquad
r_\rho(A)=1-\frac{A_{zb}^2}{A_{zz}(A_{bb}+\rho)},\quad \rho=10^{-3}.
\]
The perturbations are applied after temperature calibration, and only the declared background coordinate is profiled. The archived probability-weighted comparator divides weights by their within-group sum, giving $K_g=I_g/\bar q_g$, where $\bar q_g=\langle q\rangle_g$. Thus $r_\rho(K_g)=r_{\bar q_g\rho}(I_g)$: normalization preserves the unregularized fraction but changes the effective ridge when its numerical value is fixed. We reproduce the archived comparator separately from the predictive Fisher with fixed ridge.
