for the final manuscript. It includes the experiment implementations, retained
numerical inputs and outputs, final manuscript sources and PDF, and this
standalone supplementary document. The versioned release is available at

Use the files and checksums in this release when reproducing the reported
results. This version adopts numeric citations and harmonizes bibliography typography
and link colors in the paper and supplement; no additional evaluation is introduced. The paper and
supplement share the complete 30-entry bibliography. Scientific Appendices
A--E and the retained experimental results are unchanged. This supplement
provides a reader guide, an evidence/replay map, and the retained paired
failure-prediction intervals; it introduces no new empirical experiment.

\paragraph{Two reproduction routes.}
\emph{Numerical replay} recomputes tables and comparisons from retained outputs, without retraining image models or obtaining benchmark datasets. \emph{Independent replication} reruns the documented experiments from their original data sources. The latter requires the relevant data, dependencies, and compute resources and need not reproduce historical file bytes. These routes are documented separately in \path{START_HERE.md}, \path{docs/REPRODUCING_PRIMARY_EXPERIMENTS.md}, and \path{docs/REPRODUCING_EXPERIMENTS.md}.

\paragraph{Integrity and cohort identity.}
\path{ARTIFACT_MANIFEST.json} records the distributed files and their SHA-256 hashes, along with original archive identifiers and transformations. The corrected hidden-slice v2 cohort supplies the discovery and repair tables; it must not be replaced by the older uncorrected archive. The prospective development cohort and primary five-seed cohort remain separate. The four-way and matrix comparisons are reanalyses of retained records, not additional independently trained objective arms.

\paragraph{Historical limits and extended records.}
Some original synthetic, frozen-training, and acquisition per-seed outputs were not retained. Their aggregate reports and executable replication workflows are included, but missing historical observations and uncertainty intervals are not reconstructed. Expanded execution details are preserved in \path{docs/extended_appendix_details/}; those records retain historical numbering, while \path{docs/MANUSCRIPT_MAP.md} gives the current crosswalk. \path{docs/REBUTTAL_EXPERIMENT_MAP.md} maps the completed rebuttal additions to their sources and replay routes.
